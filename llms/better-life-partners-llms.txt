# Better Life Partners

> Better Life Partners is a Hanover, New Hampshire healthcare provider founded in 2018
> that delivers same-day, whole-person treatment for opioid and alcohol use disorder
> across New Hampshire, Maine, Massachusetts, Vermont and North Carolina. It combines
> FDA-approved medication for addiction treatment with group counseling, peer recovery
> coaching and mental health care, delivered both virtually and in person.

This file is maintained by API Evangelist (https://apievangelist.com) as a third-party
profile. Better Life Partners does not publish an `llms.txt` of its own — `/llms.txt`
returned HTTP 404 on 2026-08-07.

## API surface

Better Life Partners publishes **no API, no specification, no SDK and no developer
program**. It is a clinical services organization, not a software vendor. No
`api.`, `developer.`, `developers.`, `docs.`, `app.`, `portal.`, `status.` or `trust.`
subdomain resolves in DNS. Every OpenAPI/Swagger/GraphQL and `/.well-known/` probe
against `betterlifepartners.com` returned 404 on 2026-08-07. Do not attempt to call an
API for this company — there is not one to call.
